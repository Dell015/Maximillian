GENERAL QUESTIONS
Frequently Asked Question
01 Will I receive a booking confirmation?
Yes, once your booking is complete, you’ll receive an instant confirmation via email. Simply select your dates and preferred room type on our website, then confirm your booking by making an online payment. 
02 Can I cancel or change my booking?
Yes, on the booking confirmation email you receive, you may select the link to check your booking and cancel. Please note that we only accept cancellations up to 48 hours before your check-in time.
03 What payment methods are accepted?
You may select from many payment methods available on our site, such as via credit card, QR code, or bank transfer
04 What time is check-in and check-out?
Regular check-in time is 2pm, and regular check-out time is 12pm.
05 Is my payment information secure?
Our site is secured by redundant firewalls and we do not store any of your payment information.
