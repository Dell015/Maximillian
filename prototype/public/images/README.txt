Drop your photos in this folder, then rebuild (npm run build) or restart npm run dev.
Use these exact file names. jpg, jpeg, webp, png or avif all work.

hero.jpg                 Full-screen top of the homepage. Portrait or tall crop works best on phones
                         (it fills the whole screen), at least 1600 px on the long side. The facade at dusk is ideal.
